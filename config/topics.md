# My topics

The daily task scores every item against these topics. Edit freely: add,
remove or reorder. Higher in the list means more important.

## 1. New frontier AI models
Launches and major updates of frontier models from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google,
xAI, Meta, DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral and similar labs. Benchmarks, pricing,
context windows, availability. A major launch always makes the feed.

## 2. Claude Code, Codex and Claude news
New features, releases, changelogs, pricing or limits changes for Claude Code,
OpenAI Codex, the Claude apps and the Claude API.

## 3. General news in AI engineering
Tooling, infrastructure, protocols (MCP and similar), evals, notable
open-source releases, research with practical impact for people building with
LLMs.

## 4. Education on using Claude Code and other agents/harnesses
Skills, workflows, prompting, guides, the best ways to do things, new
techniques. Concrete and reusable beats inspirational.

## Not interested
- Clickbait, SEO listicles, "top 10 tools" posts.
- Press rewrites when the primary source exists (link the primary source instead).
- Funding rounds, valuations, hiring and executive moves, unless they change the products.
- Memes, rants, screenshots without substance, "is it just me" posts.
- AI policy and politics, unless it directly changes what I can use.
